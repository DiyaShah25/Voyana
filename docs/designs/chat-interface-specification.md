## 1. Overview & UI Architecture

The Voyana AI Assistant panel provides travelers with an interactive, contextual conversation drawer for planning trips, generating custom 3-day itineraries, finding curated destination match recommendations, and querying budget allocations and packing requirements.

---

## 2. Component Specifications & Behavior

1. **Header Navigation**
   - **Branding**: Voyana AI brand identifier with gradient sparkle avatar.
   - **Real-time Status Indicator**: Green pulsating dot indicating the AI reasoning engine is `Online`.
   - **Modal Controls**: `Minimize` / `Expand` toggle button and `Close` button.
   - **Explore Recommender Shortcut**: Quick compass button opening the Destination Recommendation drawer (**VPM-40**).

2. **Message Flow & Alternating Bubbles**
   - **User Message**: Right-aligned pill with gradient accent (`from-indigo-600 to-indigo-700`), crisp white typography, and user avatar.
   - **AI Assistant Message**: Left-aligned glassmorphic card with subtle white border, clear markdown formatting for lists and headings.
   - **AI Marking Badge**: "Generated by Voyana AI" badge with sparkle icon for transparent AI attribution.

3. **Context Citation Chips**
   - Active pill chips beneath assistant messages (e.g. `Day 1`, `Day 2`, `Packing Checklist`, `Budget Planner`, `Weather Forecast`) indicating which segments of the trip were used to formulate the advice.

4. **Action Shortcuts & Proposal Cards**
   - Integrated 1-click action buttons allowing users to open the **Budget Planner** or **Smart Packing Checklist** directly from the assistant's advice.

5. **Composer & Quick Prompts Bar**
   - Multi-line textarea supporting `Enter` to submit and `Shift+Enter` for newline.
   - Scrollable bar with pre-baked prompt pills (e.g., `"Recommend top travel destinations for me"`, `"Suggest a 3-day plan"`, `"What should we pack?"`).
