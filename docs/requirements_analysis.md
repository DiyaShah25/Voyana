## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

The objective of **VPM-16 (Analyze Requirements)** is to conduct a detailed functional and non-functional requirements decomposition from the **Voyana Business Requirements Document (BRD)**. This analysis establishes the foundational architecture, user workflows, data contracts, and feature scope for the Voyana collaborative travel platform.

---

## 2. User Personas & Target Roles

1. **Trip Organizer / Group Leader**
   - Creates new trips, configures destination dates and overall budget limits.
   - Invites collaborators, assigns packing and logistical responsibilities.
   - Approves itinerary suggestions generated by AI.

2. **Collaborating Traveler / Friend**
   - Accepts invite links, browses planned activities, and votes on options.
   - Records personal expenses, checks off assigned packing tasks.
   - Participates in real-time group chat and updates.

3. **Solo Explorer**
   - Utilizes the AI Copilot to generate customized itineraries, find hidden gems, and curate budget breakdowns.

## 3. Functional Requirements (FR) Matrix

### 3.1 AI Intelligence & Copilot Engine (`FR-AI` / `VPM-5`, `VPM-38`, `VPM-44`, `VPM-46`, `VPM-49`)
- **FR-AI-01**: Interactive AI Assistant chat drawer with real-time streaming or intelligent simulation fallback.
- **FR-AI-02**: Contextual citation chips indicating trip days, weather, budget, or packing data cited in AI answers.
- **FR-AI-03**: 1-click action proposals to apply recommendations directly to the active trip itinerary, budget, or checklist.
- **FR-AI-04**: Automated multi-day itinerary generation based on destination, traveler pace, and interest tags.

### 3.2 Trip Budgeting & Expense Splitting (`FR-BUD` / `VPM-41`, `VPM-67`, `VPM-78`)
- **FR-BUD-01**: Dynamic category allocations (Flights, Accommodations, Food & Dining, Activities, Transport, Shopping, Misc).
- **FR-BUD-02**: Multi-member expense recording with unequal/equal splits, payer selection, payment methods, and receipt notes.
- **FR-BUD-03**: Complete transaction ledger with category/member/date filtering and CSV export.
- **FR-BUD-04**: Automated debt settlement calculation using greedy debt-minimization algorithms.

### 3.3 Smart Packing & Task Assignment (`FR-PCK` / `VPM-42`, `VPM-191`)
- **FR-PCK-01**: Categorized packing checklist (Clothing, Electronics, Documents & Money, Toiletries, Essentials).
- **FR-PCK-02**: Priority tagging (`essential`, `recommended`, `optional`) and packed/unpacked progress indicators.
- **FR-PCK-03**: Member task assignment assigning items to individual travelers or 'Everyone'.
- **FR-PCK-04**: AI-powered packing generation syncing with destination climate and weather forecasts.

---

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)

- **NFR-01 Performance**: AI responses generated within < 1.5s; client UI rendered with 60 FPS transitions.
- **NFR-02 Offline Resilience**: Client-side localStorage caching for budget transactions and packing states when disconnected.
- **NFR-03 Security & Privacy**: Role-based access control (Creator, Editor, Viewer) for shared workspaces.
- **NFR-04 Usability**: Responsive glassmorphism interface adhering to modern accessibility (WCAG AA).
